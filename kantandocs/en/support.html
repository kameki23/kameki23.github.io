FAQ

How many documents are free?

Up to 3 documents in total. Editing, re-sending PDFs and recording payments are not counted. Premium (monthly) lets you issue unlimited documents without ads.

How do I change the language or currency of a document?

In the document editor, use "Language and currency". Defaults for new documents are in Settings. To change the app's own display language, open iPhone Settings → this app → Language.

How do I cancel?

iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Moving to a new iPhone?

Settings → Backup & restore → export a backup file to iCloud Drive, then restore it on the new device.

I purchased but Premium isn't active.

Tap "Restore purchases" on the plan screen while signed in with the same Apple ID.
