PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Naoki Kameda ("we") describes below how the iOS app "Easy Invoice" (かんたん請求・領収, the "App") handles your information.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or location. No account is required. On the free plan, the advertising provider described in section 5 may collect device information.

2. Data stored on your device

Your business details, clients, stamp/signature images, line items, payment records, recurring-invoice settings and generated PDFs are stored only on your device and are never sent to our servers. The number of free documents you have issued is also recorded in your Apple iCloud key-value storage.

3. Photo access

The App accesses only the photo you choose when importing a stamp or signature image. The image is processed on the device and is not sent anywhere.

4. Email, sharing and backups

When you choose to email, share or back up, the PDF or backup file is passed to the app or location you select (Mail, messaging apps, AirDrop, iCloud Drive, etc.). Those services handle data under their own policies.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free plan shows ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC). Full-screen ads are never shown while you are editing a document. Premium subscribers see no ads.

Google AdMob may collect the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, device and OS type, approximate location, and ad impressions and taps, for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. If you do not allow tracking, non-personalized ads are shown. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See Google's policy. Your document contents are never sent to the ad provider.

6. Subscriptions

Payments and subscription status are handled by Apple's In-App Purchase (StoreKit). We never receive your card details.

7. Sharing with third parties

We do not collect personal information through the App and therefore do not share it with third parties (except the advertising data described in section 5).

8. Deleting data

You can delete documents and clients in the App. Deleting the App removes all data on the device. Exported backup files can be deleted where you saved them.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Important changes will be announced on this page.
